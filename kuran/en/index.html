Kuran-ı Kerim – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Who we are

The Kuran-ı Kerim app (Android and iOS id com.ytsoft.kuran) is published by YT Soft. Contact: ytsoftbilisim@gmail.com.

Reading and bookmarks

Pages you read, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account or sign-up.

Location

Location permission is requested only when you ask for prayer times. Your approximate location is stored on your device and prayer times are calculated on the device; your location is not sent to our servers. You can revoke the permission at any time in device settings.

Audio

Recitations are streamed from the internet or saved to your device when you download them. Audio servers only see the technical information needed to deliver the file (e.g. IP address).

Ads and tracking

The app has no ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites and we do not sell your data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We do not receive or store payment details. To unlock Premium the app sends the purchase token (Android) or transaction ID (iOS) to our server (Google Firebase, EU-hosted project), which verifies it with Google Play or Apple and stores the product, status and expiry date together with a random, anonymous app ID created by Firebase Authentication. The stores notify our server when a subscription renews, expires, is cancelled or refunded.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all app data on your device. Our server only keeps the anonymous app ID and subscription status; no name, e-mail or location. To have this record deleted, write to us from the store account used for the purchase and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
