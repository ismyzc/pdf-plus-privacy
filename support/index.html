Frequently asked questions

Where are my files?

Files you create or save are stored in the app's PDF Plus folder. On iPhone and iPad you can find it in the Files app under On My iPhone › PDF Plus.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open PDF Plus, tap the crown icon and choose Restore. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone/iPad: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › PDF Plus Pro. Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

Do my documents leave my device?

No, except the text you choose to send to an AI tool (Summarize, Key points, Translate, AI Assistant). See our Privacy Policy.
