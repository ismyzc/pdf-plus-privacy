FAQ

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and tap "Restore". Make sure you are signed in with the Google account or Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Can I undo a deletion?

Deleted items go to Sweepio's trash first. Open Trash and tap "Restore" before emptying it.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All scanning, including faces, runs on your device.
