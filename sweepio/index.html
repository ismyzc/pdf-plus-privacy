Sweepio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Who we are

Sweepio: Photo Cleaner (Android and iOS id com.ytsoft.sweepio) is published by YT Soft. Contact: ytsoftbilisim@gmail.com.

Photos, videos and files

Sweepio needs access to your photos, videos, audio and files to find duplicates, similar, blurry and large items and to clean them. All analysis (duplicate detection, blur detection, face detection and grouping, scene labels and text recognition) runs on your device. Your photos, videos, files and face data are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. Items you delete go to the in-app trash first and are only removed after you confirm.

Faces

The People and "Find this face" features detect faces and compute face descriptors on your device using Google ML Kit and an on-device model. These descriptors are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, are used only to group and search your own photos, and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. They are never sent to us or third parties, and are not used for advertising or identification.

Contacts

If you use Duplicate contacts, the app reads your contacts on the device to find duplicates and merges or deletes only the ones you choose. Contacts are not uploaded.

Installed apps and usage (Android)

If you grant usage access, the app reads when your installed apps were last used and how much storage they use, only to show unused apps and their sizes. This information stays on your device.

Notifications

If you enable the weekly reminder, a local notification is created on your device. No push service is used.

Ads

Users without Premium see ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. Details: Google advertising policy. Premium users see no ads. We do not sell your data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details. To unlock Premium the app sends the purchase token (Android) or transaction ID (iOS) to our server (Google Firebase, project hosted in the EU), which verifies it with Google or Apple and stores the product, status and expiry date together with a random anonymous app ID created by Firebase Authentication. Stores notify our server when a subscription renews, expires, is cancelled or refunded.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device. Our server only keeps the anonymous app ID and subscription status. To have this record deleted, email us from the store account used for the purchase; we delete it within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
