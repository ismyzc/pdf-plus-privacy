PDF Plus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

PDF Plus: PDF Reader & Editor (package com.ytsoft.pdf_plus) is published by YT Soft. Contact: ytsoftbilisim@gmail.com.

Your files

Documents you open, edit, convert, scan or save are processed on your device. We do not upload your files to our servers and we do not have an account system. Recent files and bookmarks are stored only on your device.

AI features

When you use AI tools (Summarize, Key points, Translate, AI Assistant), the text of the selected document or your question is sent over an encrypted connection to OpenAI to generate the answer. It is not used by us for any other purpose. OpenAI's handling is described at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. If you do not use AI tools, no document text leaves your device.

Camera

The camera is used only when you scan a document. Scanned images are saved on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all app data stored on your device. Since we keep no account or server copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete. For questions write to ytsoftbilisim@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
